# Populous: The Beginning Wiki Catalog & 15-Planet Mapping

Research catalog for Wayfinder ticket [#3](https://github.com/Eclypheon/shamantogod/issues/3), synthesizing mechanics from *Populous: The Beginning* (`popre.net` and `populous.fandom.com`) and mapping them to the 15-planet incremental design defined in [`GLOSSARY.md`](../../GLOSSARY.md).

---

## 1. Spells

In *Populous: The Beginning*, permanent spells are unlocked via **Vaults of Knowledge** and charged continuously with **Mana** generated by followers and Huts. Temporary **Guest / Shot Spells** are granted by **Stone Heads** and **Obelisks** as pre-charged uses that consume no Mana.

### Permanent & Standard Spells

| Spell | Internal Mana Cost | Cast Range / Type | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Blast** | 10,000 | Short / Targeted AoE | Shockwave launching nearby friendly or enemy followers into the air; lethal if knocked into water or swamps. |
| **Convert** | 10,000 | Long / Targeted Ring | Converts neutral Wildmen within the ring into loyal Braves. |
| **Swarm** | 40,000 | Moderate / Targeted AoE | Summons stinging insects that scatter and temporarily incapacitate enemy followers. |
| **Invisibility** | ~50,000 | Moderate / Unit Buff | Renders targeted friendly units or Shaman invisible until they attack or cast. |
| **Magical Shield** | 60,000 | Moderate / Unit Buff | Grants temporary immunity to most direct spells and ranged attacks. |
| **Landbridge** | 70,000 | Moderate-Long / Terrain | Raises a strip of land out of the ocean to connect islands or landmasses. |
| **Lightning** | 80,000 | Moderate-Long / Targeted Strike | Strikes a point with lightning, killing units and damaging structures. |
| **Hypnotise** | 85,000 | Moderate / Targeted AoE | Instantly converts enemy followers within range to your tribe without a Preacher. |
| **Tornado** | 90,000 | Long / Moving Vortex | Summons a wandering vortex that flings units and tears apart buildings and trees. |
| **Swamp** | 100,000 | Moderate-Long / Terrain Hazard | Converts target land into a bog that instantly kills units and destroys buildings touching it. |
| **Flatten** | 125,000 | Moderate / Terrain | Levels hilly terrain into flat buildable land for village expansion. |
| **Erode** | High (Terrain tier) | Moderate / Terrain | Lowers target terrain step-by-step until it sinks beneath ocean level, drowning bases. |
| **Earthquake** | 175,000 | Moderate / Terrain Destruction | Fractures landmasses and collapses enemy buildings across a wide area. |
| **Firestorm** | 400,000 | Moderate / Targeted AoE | Rains continuous fire across a region, igniting buildings and panicking units. |
| **Angel of Death** | 510,000 | Global / Summon | Summons a spectral hunter that tracks down and executes enemy units across the map. |
| **Volcano** | 800,000 | Moderate / Cataclysmic Terrain | Erupts a massive volcano, spewing lava that reshapes the map and destroys everything nearby. |

### Guest / Shot-Only & Special Spells

| Spell | Internal Mana (if charged) | Cast Range / Type | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bloodlust** | 10,000 (Guest) | Long / Buff | Enrages friendly military units, boosting movement speed and combat damage. |
| **Teleport** | 10,000 (Guest) | Global / Mobility | Instantly teleports the Shaman (or selected units) to another point on the planet. |
| **Armageddon** | 10,000 (Guest) | Global / Endgame Arena | Teleports all surviving tribes into a central arena for a final battle to the death. |
| **Ghost Army** | N/A (Multiplayer) | Moderate / Decoy | Spawns illusory decoy units to distract enemy forces and absorb targeting. |

## 2. Units

All specialized followers are trained from **Braves** inside dedicated training buildings.

| Unit | Training Source | Primary Role | Strengths & Mechanics | Counters & Weaknesses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wildman** | Neutral spawns | Pre-conversion population | Roam neutral areas; converted into Braves via **Convert**. | Cannot fight or build; vulnerable to all combat units. |
| **Brave** | Huts / Converted Wildmen | Economy & construction | Chops wood, builds/upgrades Huts and training buildings, staffs structures, prays at worship sites. | Weak in melee; defeated by Warriors, Fire Warriors, and Preachers. |
| **Preacher** | Temple | Conversion & anti-conversion | Channels sermons to convert enemy followers and huts; blocks enemy Preachers; immune to conversion. | Outranged by Fire Warriors; vulnerable to melee Warriors and direct spells. |
| **Warrior** | Warrior Training Hut | Frontline melee & siege | High HP and melee damage; tears down enemy buildings; generates small passive mana; sounds alarm in Guard Towers. | Vulnerable to ranged Fire Warriors, Swarm, Blast/water knockback, and Swamp. |
| **Fire Warrior** | Firewarrior Training Hut | Ranged fire & knockback | Fires explosive fireballs from range; knocks enemies into water; can fire from Boats and Balloons. | Fragile in melee if closed down by Warriors; vulnerable to Magical Shielded rushers. |
| **Spy** | Spy Training Hut | Infiltration & sabotage | Disguises as enemy tribe units; sneaks into enemy settlements to burn down huts silently. | Weakest combat unit (loses to a Brave); unmasked by units in Guard Towers or manual inspection. |
| **Shaman** | Reincarnation Site | Tribal avatar & spellcaster | Casts all spells; respawns at Reincarnation Site if killed while base stands. | Physically weaker than Warriors; losing her temporarily halts spellcasting. |

## 3. Buildings

Buildings are constructed by Braves using harvested wood (trees).

| Building | Wood Cost | Function & Progression |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Small Hut (Lv 1)** | 3 wood | Base dwelling; +3 population cap (base cap starts at 5); houses up to 3 Braves; generates Mana. |
| **Medium Hut (Lv 2)** | 4 wood (upgrade) | +6 total pop cap (+3 net); houses up to 4 Braves; higher Mana output. |
| **Large Hut (Lv 3)** | 5 wood (upgrade) | +9 total pop cap (+3 net); houses up to 5 Braves; maximum hut Mana output. |
| **Warrior Training Hut** | 5 wood | Converts Braves into **Warriors**. |
| **Temple** | 8 wood | Converts Braves into **Preachers**. |
| **Firewarrior Training Hut** | 8 wood | Converts Braves into **Fire Warriors**. |
| **Spy Training Hut** | 8 wood | Converts Braves into **Spies**. |
| **Guard Tower** | 5 wood | Buildable anywhere (no adjacency needed); extends look-out/attack/conversion range of garrisoned unit; Spies inside unmask enemy Spies. |
| **Boat Hut** | 5 wood | Built on coastlines; automatically constructs **Boats** for amphibious transport across water. |
| **Balloon Hut** | 11 wood | Late-game structure; constructs **Balloons** for aerial troop transport bypassing water and land defenses. |

## 4. Rival Tribes & 25-Planet Campaign Themes

In the original 25-planet campaign, the player (Blue tribe) faces three distinct rival tribes:

- **Dakini (Red):** Aggressive, expansionist melee-heavy faction; dominant early-to-mid campaign rival.
- **Matak (Green):** Defensive terrain-control faction; favors **Swamp** and **Swarm** to trap invaders.
- **Chumara (Yellow):** Island/archipelago specialists; relies heavily on **Preachers** and naval positioning.

### Campaign Arc (25 Planets Condensed by Theme)

1. **Early Shaman Foothold (Orig. 1-5):** *The Journey Begins*, *Night Falls*, *Crisis of Faith*, *Combined Forces*, *Death From Above* — introduces Convert, Blast, Huts, Warriors, Preachers, and Fire Warriors against single tribes.
2. **Terrain & Amphibious Expansion (Orig. 6-12):** *Building Bridges*, *Unseen Enemy*, *Continental Divide*, *Fire in the Mist*, *From the Depths*, *Treacherous Souls*, *An Easy Target* — introduces Landbridge, Boats, Spies, Swamp, and multi-island assaults.
3. **Aerial & Multi-Tribe Escalation (Orig. 13-19):** *Aerial Bombardment*, *Attacked From All Sides*, *Incarcerated*, *Bloodlust*, *Middle Ground*, *Head Hunter*, *Unlikely Allies* — introduces Balloons, Bloodlust, three-way wars (Dakini + Chumara + Matak), and temporary alliances.
4. **Cataclysm & Divinity (Orig. 20-25):** *Archipelago*, *Fractured Earth*, *Solo*, *Inferno*, *Journey's End*, *The Beginning* — features Erode, Earthquake, Firestorm, Volcano, Angel of Death, and Armageddon leading to godhood.

## 5. Proposed Mapping to Our 15-Planet Incremental Game

Per [`GLOSSARY.md`](../../GLOSSARY.md), **Planets 1-11** are **Shaman planets** (walkable avatar, finite cast range), and **Planets 12-15** are **Junior God planets** (unlimited planet-wide cast range, finite Mana, escalating Endless Swarms). Reaching **>95% Dominance** conquers a planet; earlier planets remain active for passive Mana and Divine Essence via **Endless Swarm**.

### Core Incremental Adaptations

- **Economy Simplification:** Replace manual wood chopping with **Mana** + **Auto-build** from Planet 1 (`GLOSSARY.md`). Braves automatically construct and upgrade Huts (Lv 1 -> Lv 2 -> Lv 3) and staff training buildings according to **Unit-ratio sliders**.
- **Unit Roster Focus:** Core sliders cover **Brave**, **Preacher**, **Warrior**, and **Fire Warrior** (`GLOSSARY.md`). **Spy** and **Guard Tower** can either unlock as mid-game tactical/automation upgrades (e.g., passive sabotage / range-extension turret) or remain optional Planet 5+ unlocks without cluttering the 4-slider UI.
- **Transport (Boat / Balloon):** Instead of micromanaged ferrying, Boat Huts and Balloon Huts can unlock automated amphibious/aerial raid routes on archipelago/fortress planets (Planets 5-10), letting Warriors and Fire Warriors cross water/cliffs automatically.
- **Spell Scaling:** Map internal Mana costs to incremental Mana tiers and convert **Shot Spells** (Stone Heads / Obelisks) into periodic free charges or cooldown-based Shaman abilities, while **Vaults of Knowledge** serve as permanent planet-milestone spell unlocks.
