# Glossary

Domain terms for Shaman to God. Every term here describes game concepts, not code or implementation.

## Solar system and progression

- **Solar system**: The campaign map (the Kaelis System) of 15 planets that the player must conquer to win the game.
- **Planet**: A single playable world with its own terrain, enemy tribes, and dominance progress. Planets 1 to 11 are Shaman planets; Planets 12 to 15 are Junior God planets.
- **Dominance %**: Equal combination of loyal population share (50%) and enemy structures destroyed (50%) on a planet. Reaching above 95% conquers the planet.
- **Endless swarm**: Continuous waves of enemy units that keep spawning on a planet even after 95% Dominance, allowing players to revisit earlier planets to scale passive Mana and destruction.
- **Prestige**: Advancing from the current planet to the next planet in the solar system. Prestiging resets the active Shaman and village on the new planet, keeps Meta-Progression upgrades, and leaves earlier planets generating passive Mana and destruction.
- **Meta-progression**: Permanent upgrades purchased with Divine Essence across planets that carry over through every Prestige.
- **Divine essence**: Meta-currency earned from planet Dominance % and Endless Swarm destruction, spent on permanent upgrades across Prestiges.

## Roles and units

- **Shaman**: The player's avatar on Planets 1 to 11. She walks across the planet and has a limited cast range for spells.
- **Junior God**: The player's ascended state from Planet 12 onward. Cast range is unlimited across the planet, though Mana remains finite and enemy swarms can still overwhelm under-prepared forces.
- **Follower**: A villager loyal to the player's tribe, converted with Mana or spawned from Huts.
- **Brave**: An untrained base follower who gathers, builds Huts, and staffs training buildings.
- **Preacher**: A trained follower who converts enemy followers to the player's tribe.
- **Warrior**: A trained melee follower with weapons who fights enemy units and destroys structures.
- **Fire warrior**: A specialized ranged follower who shoots fireballs at enemies and buildings.

## Buildings and economy

- **Hut**: A residential building automatically constructed by Braves that increases population capacity and spawns new Braves over time.
- **Auto-build**: Follower automation active from Planet 1 where Braves construct Huts and staff training buildings according to the player's unit-ratio sliders.
- **Unit-ratio slider**: A control setting the target proportion of Braves, Preachers, Warriors, and Fire Warriors that followers automatically train toward.
- **Training building**: A specialized structure (such as a Temple, Warrior Training Hut, or Fire Warrior Training Hut) that converts Braves into Preachers, Warriors, or Fire Warriors.
- **Mana**: The base currency generated by followers, Huts, and conquered planets, spent to convert initial followers, build structures, train units, and cast Shaman spells.
- **Stone head**: A cooldown-based monument or ability slot that grants free shot-spells (Bloodlust, Teleport, Armageddon, Ghost Army) without spending Mana.
- **Alliance**: A diplomatic pact with a compatible rival tribe on multi-tribe planets that sends joint squads, shares passive Mana, and counts toward Dominance %.
